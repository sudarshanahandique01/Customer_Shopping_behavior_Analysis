This dashboard presents a clear overview of customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and revenue performance. It highlights key metrics such as average purchase amount, total number of customers, and average review rating to give a quick understanding of business performance. The visuals show how customers are distributed based on subscription status and how sales and revenue vary across different product categories. Interactive filters like gender, category, and shipping type allow deeper analysis of customer preferences and trends. Overall, the dashboard helps identify high-performing categories, understand customer engagement, and support better business decisions in a simple and interactive way
